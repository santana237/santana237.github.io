Chess Master Pro

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Chess Master Pro has no account and no server of its own. Your settings and game progress are stored on your device, and the Stockfish chess engine runs on your device.

Game Center: if you use it, Apple processes your leaderboards and achievements under its own privacy policy.

Advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process your IP address and device identifiers under its own privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. Questions: support@vsptech.host.
