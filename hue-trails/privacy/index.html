Privacy policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Hue Trails is a puzzle game by VSPTECH. The puzzles work offline. This policy explains what the game stores and what its advertising and store services may process.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your garden progress, earned stars, selected color palette, soundtrack preference, and haptic preference in local app storage on your device. We do not receive or have access to this information. The game does not require an account and works without an internet connection.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and requests non-personalized ads. When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may process device information, an advertising identifier where available, IP address, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. Where required, the game presents Google's consent form and offers advertising privacy choices in Settings. The one-time Quiet Garden Pass removes in-game ads.

Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes purchases and provides the game with purchase and restore status. We do not receive payment card details. Purchase records are handled under the relevant store provider's privacy policy.

Information we do not use

We do not operate player accounts or game analytics, and do not collect gameplay progress on our servers. The game does not request location, contacts, or photos, and has no social sign-in.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the address and message content you choose to send. We use that information only to respond to your request. Your email provider handles delivery and may retain its own records under its privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Game preferences and progress remain in local app storage until you erase progress in the game’s Settings or uninstall the game. Advertising and purchase records are handled by Google and the relevant store provider under their policies. If you email support, you can ask us to delete your support message by replying to the same conversation. Device backup and restore features are controlled by your device platform.

Children

The game does not ask players to create an account or submit personal information. The free version contains third-party advertising. Parents can use platform controls to manage purchases and advertising settings for their device.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game’s information practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email support@vsptech.host.
