Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

SecureRSA encrypts and decrypts text with RSA on your device. It has no account and no server of its own: your keys and messages are not sent to us.

Sharing: when you share a key or an encrypted message, it goes through the app you choose.

Camera: used only to scan QR codes.

Advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process your IP address and device identifiers under its own privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device. Questions: support@vsptech.host.
